Brief Description (RRB NTPC Inter Level Recruitment 2025)

The Government of India, Ministry of Railways, through the Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs), has released the Centralised Employment Notice (CEN) No. 07/2025 for the recruitment of 3058 vacancies for various Non-Technical Popular Categories (NTPC) - Under Graduate (10+2) posts. Eligible candidates with a 12th (+2 Stage) qualification can apply online for posts like Commercial Cum Ticket Clerk, Accounts Clerk cum Typist, and others. Applications must be submitted online only through the official RRB websites by 27.11.2025.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

  • Candidates must have passed 12th (10+2 Stage) or its equivalent with not less than 50% marks in the aggregate from a recognized board.
  • The 50% marks requirement is NOT insisted upon for candidates from SC, ST, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen, OR for candidates who possess qualifications higher than 12th (10+2 Stage) (e.g., Graduation, Post Graduation).
  • For posts of Accounts Clerk cum Typist and Junior Clerk cum Typist, typing proficiency in English/Hindi on a computer is essential.
  • Important: Candidates awaiting the final results of their prescribed educational qualification are NOT eligible to apply.

Selection Process

The recruitment process will involve 1st Stage CBT, 2nd Stage CBT, Computer Based Typing Skill Test (CBTST) (as applicable), followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination.

How to Apply- RRB NTPC Inter Level Recruitment 2025

  1. Candidates must apply online through the official website of any RRB.
  2. Candidates must first 'Create an Account' on the RRB portal or log in with existing credentials.
  3. Important: Candidates must choose only ONE RRB. Multiple applications to different or the same RRB will lead to rejection and debarment.
  4. During the application, a Live Photo of the candidate will be captured via webcam or mobile camera. Pre-existing photos cannot be uploaded.
  5. Upload a scanned copy of your signature as per the specifications.
  6. Pay the application fee online and submit the form.
  7. Take a printout of the submitted application form for your records.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) for RRB NTPC CEN 07/2025

1. What is the last date to apply for RRB NTPC (10+2) CEN 07/2025?

The last date to submit the online application is 27th November 2025 (23:59 hrs).

2. What is the educational qualification needed?

You must have passed 12th (+2 Stage) or its equivalent. A minimum of 50% marks is required, but this is waived for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-S candidates and those with higher qualifications (like a graduate degree).

3. Can I apply if I am in my final year of 12th?

No. The notification clearly states that candidates awaiting the final results of their prescribed educational qualification are not eligible to apply.

4. Can I apply to multiple RRBs?

No. A candidate must submit an application to only one RRB. Submitting more than one application will lead to immediate rejection of all applications and debarment.

5. How is the application fee refunded?

For General candidates, ₹400 (out of ₹500) is refunded. For reserved categories (SC/ST/Women/PwBD, etc.), the full ₹250 is refunded (bank charges deducted). This refund is only processed for candidates who actually appear for the 1st Stage CBT.
