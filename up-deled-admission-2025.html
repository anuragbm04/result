Examination Regulatory Authority UP, Prayagraj - Online Registration Form

Brief Description

Examination Regulatory Authority, Uttar Pradesh has released the notification for UP DELED (formerly BTC) Training 2025. Candidates who wish to become teachers in primary schools can apply online. The selection will be based on Merit (10th, 12th, and Graduation marks).

Important Dates

Application Begin24 November 2025
Last Date for Registration15 December 2025
Pay Fee Last Date16 December 2025
Complete Form / Print18 December 2025

*Note: Registration starts from afternoon on 24/11/2025.

Application Fee

General / OBC₹ 700/-
SC / ST₹ 500/-
PH (Divyang)₹ 200/-
  • Pay via SBI Internet Banking, Debit/Credit Cards, or E-Challan.

Eligibility Criteria (2025)

Educational Qualification

  • Bachelor's Degree in any stream (Art, Science, Commerce, etc.) from a recognized University.
  • Minimum 50% marks for General/OBC candidates.
  • Minimum 45% marks for SC/ST candidates.

Age Limit

  • Minimum Age: 18 Years
  • Maximum Age: 35 Years
  • (Age Relaxation extra as per UP Government Rules).

How to Apply for UP DELED 2025

  1. Visit Official Website: Go to the official website https://updeled.gov.in.
  2. Registration: Click on "Candidate Registration Part-1". Read the guidelines carefully and fill in your basic details.
  3. Verify Details (Crucial): Before clicking "Final Save", cross-check every entry with your original documents. Note: No opportunity for correction will be given after the final save.
  4. Pay Application Fee: After registration, use the "Pay Application Fee" link to submit the required amount via SBI MOPS (Net Banking/Card).
  5. Final Submission: After fee submission, fill in the remaining details in Part-2 of the form, upload your photo and signature, and submit the final application.
  6. Print Application: Take a printout of the final completed application form by 18.12.2025 for future reference.

Instructions for Candidates

  • No Correction Allowed: Candidates will NOT get any chance to correct their application after final submission. Please verify all entries with your original documents before "Final Save".
  • Reservation: Candidates from states other than Uttar Pradesh will be treated as Unreserved (General) category and will not get any reservation benefits.
  • Mode of Application: Applications are accepted via Online mode only. No other mode is accepted.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. When will the UP DELED 2025 online application start?

The online registration starts on 24 November 2025 (from the afternoon).

2. What is the last date to apply for UP BTC 2025?

The last date for online registration is 15 December 2025. You must pay the fee by 16 December 2025.

3. Can I correct mistakes in my application form later?

No. The official notification clearly states that no opportunity for correction will be given after final submission. You must verify your entries with original documents before saving.

4. Can candidates from other states apply for UP DELED?

Yes, candidates from other states can apply, but they will be treated as Unreserved (General) category candidates and will not receive any reservation benefits.

5. What is the official website for UP DELED 2025?

The only official website for registration and fee payment is https://updeled.gov.in.
