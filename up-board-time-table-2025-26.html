UPMSP Class 10th and 12th Exam Date Sheet 2026

Brief Description (UP Board Class 10th & Class 12th Examination 2026)

The Office of the Secretary, Madhyamik Shiksha Parishad, Uttar Pradesh (UPMSP), Prayagraj, has officially announced the board exam time table for the year 2026. The examinations for both High School (Class 10th) and Intermediate (Class 12th) will be conducted simultaneously. As per the notification, the exams will commence on **18 February 2026** and will conclude on **12 March 2026**. Candidates can download the detailed subject-wise PDF of the date sheet.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. When will the UP Board Exam 2026 start?

The UP Board exams for both Class 10th and 12th will start on 18th February 2026.

2. What is the last date for the UP Board 2026 exam?

The final exam for the UP Board will be held on 12th March 2026.

3. What are the timings for the UP Board exams?

Exams are held in two shifts. The Morning Shift is from 8:30 AM to 11:45 AM, and the Evening Shift is from 2:00 PM to 5:15 PM.

4. When is the Class 10th Mathematics (Ganit) exam?

The Class 10th Mathematics exam is on 27th February 2026, in the morning shift (8:30 AM - 11:45 AM).

5. When is the Class 12th Physics (Bhautik Vigyan) exam?

The Class 12th Physics exam is on 27th February 2026, in the evening shift (2:00 PM - 5:15 PM).

6. When is the Class 12th Chemistry (Rasayan Vigyan) exam?

The Class 12th Chemistry exam is on 25th February 2026, in the evening shift (2:00 PM - 5:15 PM).
