How to Check MPESB Group 4 Result 2025

  1. Click on the "Download Scorecard" link provided above.
  2. You will be redirected to the official MPESB Result portal.
  3. Enter your Application Number (13 digits) OR Roll Number (8 digits).
  4. Enter your Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY).
  5. Enter the TAC Code (printed on your Admit Card).
  6. Solve the simple arithmetic problem (e.g., 45 + 10) and click Search.
  7. Your Result and Merit position will be displayed on the screen. Print it for future reference.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. When was the MP Group 4 Result 2025 declared?

The result was officially declared on 25 November 2025.

2. What details are needed to check the result?

You need your Application Number or Roll Number, Date of Birth, and the TAC Code (from Admit Card).

3. What is the TAC Code?

TAC Code is a unique code printed on your Exam Admit Card. It is mandatory for downloading the result.

4. Is the Merit List released?

Yes, the scorecard includes your marks as well as your waiting/merit position in the respective category.
