Passing Marks (Qualifying)

Candidates must secure the following minimum percentage to qualify:

  • General: 50%
  • BC (Backward Class): 45.5%
  • EBC (Extremely Backward Class): 42.5%
  • SC / ST: 40%
  • Divyang (PH): 40%
  • Female (All Categories): 40%

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What are the passing marks for Bihar STET 2025?

The qualifying marks are 50% for General, 45.5% for BC, 42.5% for EBC, and 40% for SC/ST/Divyang/Female candidates.

2. Is there negative marking in the STET Exam?

No, there is no negative marking for wrong answers in the eligibility test.

3. How is the final score calculated?

Since the CBT exam is held in multiple shifts with different difficulty levels, the result will be prepared using the Normalization methodYour qualification depends on the Normalized Score, not the Raw Score.

4. What was the duration of the exam?

The Computer Based Test (CBT) was conducted for a duration of 150 minutes (2 hours 30 minutes).
