Brief Description

Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) is conducting the Assistant Teacher (LT Grade) Recruitment Exam 2025 to fill vacancies in Government Inter Colleges (GIC). The exam will be conducted in offline mode (OMR Based) across various districts of Uttar Pradesh starting from December 06, 2025.

How to Download Admit Card

  1. Click on the "Download Admit Card" link given below.
  2. You will be redirected to the UPPSC Official Website.
  3. Enter your OTR Number (One Time Registration) or Registration No.
  4. Select your Date of Birth and Gender.
  5. Enter the Captcha Code and click on "Download Admit Card".
  6. Print two copies of the Hall Ticket and read the instructions carefully.

Documents Required at Exam Center

  • Printout of Admit Card (Hard Copy).
  • Two Passport Size Photographs (same as uploaded).
  • Original Photo ID Proof (Aadhar Card / PAN Card / Voter ID / DL).
  • Black Ball Point Pen.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How can I download UPPSC LT Grade Admit Card?

You can download it from the official website uppsc.up.nic.in using your OTR number and DOB.

2. What is the exam date for UP LT Grade Social Science?

The Social Science exam is scheduled for 17 January 2026 in the Morning Shift.

3. Is OTR mandatory for downloading the Admit Card?

Yes, as per new UPPSC guidelines, the OTR number is required for all candidate login activities.

4. Will there be negative marking in the exam?

Yes, there is usually a negative marking of 0.33 (1/3rd) marks for every wrong answer.
