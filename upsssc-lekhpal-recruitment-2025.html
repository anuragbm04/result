UPSSSC Lekhpal Recruitment 2025

Advt No. 02-Exam/2025: Rajasva Lekhpal Main Examination

Last Updated: Loading...

🔥 Sangam Result Update: UP Lekhpal Notification Out

Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) has released the notification for 7994 Lekhpal posts. Online applications start from 29 December 2025.

Brief Description

UPSSSC invites online applications from eligible candidates for the selection of **Rajasva Lekhpal** (7994 Posts). Candidates who have appeared in **UP PET 2025** and have a valid score card are eligible to apply for the Main Examination. The selection will be based on a written exam consisting of General Knowledge, Hindi, Rural Development, and Computer Knowledge.

  • Intermediate (10+2): Passed from UP Board or any recognized board.

Detailed Syllabus (New)

Part 1: General Subjects (65 Marks)

  • General Hindi: Grammar, Sandhi, Samas, Tatsam/Tadbhav, Synonyms/Antonyms, Idioms, Sentence Correction, Gender, Tense.
  • Mathematics / DI: Data Interpretation (Graphs, Charts, Statistical Analysis).
  • General Studies: History, Polity, Geography, Economy, General Science.
  • Rural Development: Rural Schemes (Central/State), Land Reforms, Rural Healthcare, Social Development.
  • Environment: Ecology, Biodiversity, Pollution, Disaster Management.

Part 2: Computer & IT (15 Marks)

  • Basic Computer Knowledge, Hardware/Software, Operating Systems.
  • MS Word, MS Excel, Internet, Email, WWW.
  • **New Topics:** AI, Big Data, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, IoT, Cyber Security, Digital Financial Tools, E-Governance.

Part 3: UP Special GK (20 Marks)

  • History, Culture, Art, Festivals, Folk Dances of Uttar Pradesh.
  • UP Geography, Climate, Soil, Agriculture, Industry.
  • UP Govt Schemes, Economy, Polity, and Current Affairs of UP.
