Brief Information

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) has declared the result for the Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2025. Candidates who appeared for the exam can now download their scorecard. The PET Score is valid for 1 year from the date of result declaration and is mandatory for upcoming Group C vacancies like Lekhpal, VDO, Junior Assistant, and ANM.

How to Download UPSSSC PET Result 2025

  1. Click on the "Download Score Card" link provided above.
  2. You will be redirected to the official result portal (upsssc.gov.in).
  3. Enter your Registration Number.
  4. Select your Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY).
  5. Select your Gender.
  6. Enter the Verification Code (Captcha) shown on the screen.
  7. Click on the green "See Result" button.
  8. Your Score Card displaying Absolute Score, Normalized Score, and Percentile will appear. Print it for future reference.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How long is the UPSSSC PET 2025 Score valid?

The PET 2025 score is typically valid for one year from the date of the result declaration.

2. Which marks are used for shortlisting: Absolute or Normalized?

For shortlisting in mains exams (like Lekhpal), the Normalized Score is usually considered.

3. I forgot my Registration Number. How can I check the result?

You can retrieve your Registration Number by clicking on "Know Your Registration Number" on the UPSSSC official website using your Name, Father's Name, and DOB.
