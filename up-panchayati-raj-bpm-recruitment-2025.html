Brief Description

The Panchayati Raj Department, Uttar Pradesh has released a notification for the recruitment of 826 Block Project Managers (BPM) under the Rashtriya Gram Swaraj Abhiyan (RGSA) scheme. The recruitment will be conducted through outsourcing agencies (M/s Rajdeep Enterprises & M/s World Class Services Ltd). Interested candidates must be a domicile of the district they are applying for. Applications are accepted only via the Sewayojan Portal.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

  • Graduate Degree in any stream with minimum 60% Marks.
  • PLUS CCC Certificate from NIELIT OR Higher Computer Course (O Level, A Level, DCA, PGDCA, ADCA).
  • OR: Technical Graduates (BCA, B.Tech, MCA, B.Sc IT) with minimum 60% marks are also eligible (No separate CCC required).

Domicile Requirement

  • Candidate MUST be a resident (Domicile) of the same district where they are applying for the vacancy.

Selection Process

The recruitment is through outsourcing agencies via the Sewayojan Portal. The selection will involve:

  • Shortlisting: Candidates will be shortlisted based on their Educational Qualification (Merit) applied through the portal.
  • Interview / Skill Test: Shortlisted candidates will be called for an Interview by the outsourcing agency.
  • Document Verification: Original documents (Mark sheets, Domicile, Caste Certificate) will be verified before final joining.
  • * Note: There is typically no written exam for Sewayojan outsourcing jobs unless specified by the agency.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is the salary of UP Panchayat Block Project Manager?

The fixed honorarium is ₹15,001 per month.

2. Can I apply for other districts?

No. The notification explicitly states that the candidate must be a domicile (resident) of the same district where they are applying.

3. Is CCC mandatory for B.Tech/BCA students?

No. If you have a Technical Degree like BCA, B.Tech, MCA, or B.Sc IT with 60% marks, you do not need a separate CCC certificate.

4. What is the selection mode?

Selection is based on Merit followed by an Interview conducted by the outsourcing agency.
