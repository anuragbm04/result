UPPSC Various Post Recruitment 2025

Advt No. D-6/E-1/2025: Veterinary Officer, Drug Inspector & Others

Last Updated: Loading...

🔥 Sangam Result Update: UPPSC Notification 2025 Out

Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) has released the notification for multiple Gazetted posts. OTR is mandatory. Online applications start from 22 December 2025.

Brief Description

UPPSC invites online applications for direct recruitment to various posts including **Veterinary Officer**, **Vetting Officer**, **Swasthya Shiksha Adhikari**, **Drug Inspector**, and **Dental Surgeon**. Candidates must complete One Time Registration (OTR) 72 hours before applying. The selection may include a Screening Examination followed by an Interview.

Exam Pattern & Selection Process

The selection process may include a **Screening Examination (Objective Type)** followed by an Interview. The final merit will be determined by adding **75% marks of Screening Test** and **25% marks of Interview**.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is the last date to apply for UPPSC Various Posts?

The last date for fee payment and submission is **22 January 2026**.

2. Is OTR mandatory for UPPSC Recruitment 2025?

Yes, candidates must obtain an OTR Number **72 hours before** applying.

3. Is there negative marking in the UPPSC Screening Exam?

Yes, **0.33 (1/3rd)** marks will be deducted for each wrong answer.

4. What is the age limit for these posts?

The age limit is **21 to 40 years** as of July 1, 2025.
