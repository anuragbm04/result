Brief Information

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has officially declared the result of the Computer Based Examination for Stenographer Grade 'C' & 'D' Examination, 2025. Candidates who appeared in the exam can now check their qualifying status for the next stage (Skill Test) by downloading the List 1 (Grade C) and List 2 (Grade D) PDFs.

How to Check Result

  1. Click on the "Grade C" or "Grade D" Result PDF link above.
  2. A PDF file containing the Roll Numbers and Names of qualified candidates will open.
  3. Press Ctrl + F (or use the Search option on mobile) and enter your Roll Number.
  4. If your Roll Number is highlighted, you have qualified for the Skill Test.
  5. Save the PDF for future reference.
