RSSB Upper Primary School Teacher (Level-2) Direct Recruitment 2025

Sanskrit Education Department (Class 6 to 8) - Advt No. 09/2025

Brief Description (RSSB REET MAINS UPPER TEACHER RECRUITMENT)

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) has invited online applications for the recruitment of 2123 Upper Primary Teachers (Level-2) in the Sanskrit Education Department. Candidates who have qualified REET Level-2 and possess the required B.Ed/D.El.Ed qualification can apply for subjects like Maths-Science, Social Science (SST), English, Hindi, and Sanskrit. The online application window opens on 7th November 2025.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification (General)

  • REET Qualified: Must have passed Rajasthan Eligibility Examination for Teacher (REET 2022 or 2025) in Level-2.
  • Graduation: Graduate with at least 50% marks in the relevant subject.
  • B.Ed / D.El.Ed: Bachelor in Education (B.Ed) OR 2-Year Diploma in Elementary Education.

Subject Specific Requirements

  • Maths/Science: Graduation with at least one subject from Chemistry, Physics, Botany, Biology, Micro-Biology, Bio-Tech, Bio-Chem, or Maths.
  • Social Studies: Graduation with at least one subject from History, Geography, Economics, Pol. Science, Sociology, Public Admin, Philosophy, Accountancy, etc.
  • Sanskrit: Shastri (with 50% marks) or Varishtha Upadhyaya + B.Ed/D.El.Ed.

  • Educational Methodology (20 Marks)
  • Educational Psychology (20 Marks)
  • Information Technology (10 Marks)

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) for RSSB Level-2 Teacher 2025

1. What is the last date to apply for RSSB Level-2 Recruitment 2025?

The last date to submit the online application is 06 December 2025.

2. How many total vacancies are there for Level-2?

There are a total of 2123 vacancies for Upper Primary Teachers in the Sanskrit Education Department.

3. Is REET qualification mandatory?

Yes, candidates must have passed REET Level-2 (2022 or 2025) with the required minimum percentage to be eligible.

4. Can I apply for multiple subjects?

Yes, if you meet the eligibility criteria for more than one subject, you must submit a separate application for each subject.

5. What is the exam date for RSSB Level-2?

The exam is scheduled to be held between 17 January 2026 and 21 January 2026.

6. Is there negative marking in the exam?

Yes, there is a negative marking of 1/3rd mark for every incorrect answer.

7. Which subject has the highest vacancies?

The Maths & Science subject has the highest number of vacancies, totaling 1043 posts (970 Non-TSP + 73 TSP).

8. Can final year students apply?

Yes, candidates appearing in the final year of their qualification can apply, provided they acquire the qualification before the exam date.

9. What is the application fee for OBC Non-Creamy Layer?

The application fee for OBC (Non-Creamy Layer), EWS, SC, and ST candidates is ₹ 400/-.

10. Is this recruitment for the Sanskrit Department or General Department?

This specific notification (Advt No. 09/2025) is for the Sanskrit Education Department, but it covers general subjects like Maths, Science, and SST as well.
