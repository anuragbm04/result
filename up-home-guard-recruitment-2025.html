UP Home Guard Volunteer Recruitment 2025

Uttar Pradesh Home Guards Enrollment - Total 41,424 Vacancies

Last Updated: Loading...

Brief Description

The Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPRPB) has released the notification for the enrollment of Home Guard Volunteers (Swayamsevak). A total of 41,424 vacancies are available across 75 districts of Uttar Pradesh. Candidates who have passed Class 10th (High School) and are residents of the respective district can apply online from 18th November 2025 to 17th December 2025.

Exam Pattern & Syllabus 2025

The written examination will be conducted in **Pen and Paper (OMR Based)** mode. The question paper will consist of **Objective Type** multiple-choice questions.

SubjectGeneral Knowledge
Total Questions100
Total Marks100
Time Duration2 Hours (120 Mins)

Detailed Syllabus (General Knowledge)

The question paper will cover the following topics:

  • General Science: Basic scientific concepts.
  • History: History of India.
  • Constitution & Polity: Indian Constitution, Indian Economy, and Culture.
  • Agriculture & Commerce: Indian Agriculture, Commerce, Trade, Population, Environment, and Urbanization.
  • Geography: Geography of India and World Geography.
  • Uttar Pradesh Special: Natural Resources of UP, Education, Culture, Social Practices of UP, Revenue, Police, and General Administration system in UP.
  • Current Affairs: National and International importance, Organizations, Demonetization and its impact.
  • Human Rights & Security: Human Rights, Internal Security, Terrorism, India and its neighbors.
  • Miscellaneous: Awards and Honors, Countries/Capitals/Currencies, Important Days, Discoveries, Books and Authors, Social Media Communication, Cyber Crime, GST.

How to Apply Online

The application process involves multiple steps including One Time Registration (OTR). Follow the instructions below carefully:

  1. Step 1: One Time Registration (OTR): Candidates must first register on the UPPRPB OTR platform using a unique Email ID and Mobile Number. DigiLocker verification is required during OTR.
  2. Step 2: Login: After OTR, login to the portal using your OTR Number, Aadhaar, or DigiLocker credentials.
  3. Step 3: Fill Application: Fill in your personal details, educational qualifications, and select the district (Domicile District).
  4. Step 4: Live Photograph:
    • The system will capture a Real-Time (Live) Photo via webcam.
    • Ensure you are not wearing a cap, mask, or dark glasses.
    • The background should be plain and well-lit.
    • Do NOT wear any uniform while taking the photo.
  5. Step 5: Upload Signature:
    • Upload a scanned signature on white paper with Black Ink.
    • Size: 30KB to 50KB (JPG/JPEG).
    • Dimensions: 50mm x 20mm.
  6. Step 6: Upload Documents:
    • Candidates are advised to upload documents (Mark sheets, Caste Certificate, Domicile) via DigiLocker.
    • If documents are not on DigiLocker, scan and upload them manually.
  7. Step 7: Fee Payment: Pay the application fee (₹400 for Gen/OBC/EWS, ₹300 for SC/ST) online via Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card, or UPI.
  8. Step 8: Final Submission: Preview the application form and click "Submit". Take a printout of the final application for future reference.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is the last date to apply for UP Home Guard 2025?

The last date to submit the online application is 17th December 2025.

2. Can candidates from other states apply?

No. Candidates must be a Domicile (Resident) of the specific district in Uttar Pradesh for which they are applying.

3. What is the salary (Duty Allowance) for UP Home Guard?

As per the notification, the current duty allowance is ₹ 600/- per day plus dearness allowance.

4. Is there any physical test?

Yes, candidates must clear the Physical Efficiency Test (Running). Male: 4.8 km in 28 mins, Female: 2.4 km in 16 mins.

5. What is the qualification for Home Guard?

Candidates must have passed Class 10th (High School) from a recognized board.
