MPESB Group 2, Subgroup 3 Various Posts Recruitment 2025

Joint Recruitment Test-2025 for the posts of Group-2, Subgroup-3

Brief Description

The Madhya Pradesh Karmachari Chayan Mandal (MPESB), Bhopal, has released the notification for the Group 2, Subgroup 3 Joint Recruitment Test 2025. This recruitment drive aims to fill 454 vacancies for various posts, including Junior Supply Officer, Assistant Engineer (Civil), Sanitation Inspector, and other technical and non-technical positions. Eligible candidates with a graduate degree and a valid MP Employment Office registration can apply online via the official ESB portal from 29.10.2025 to 12.11.2025.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

  • Candidates must have a Bachelor's Degree (Graduate) in the relevant discipline for the specific post they are applying for (e.g., B.Sc. in Biology/Agri for Silk Inspector, B.E. in Civil for AE Civil).
  • Mandatory: All candidates must have a live (valid) registration with the Madhya Pradesh State Employment Office (मध्यप्रदेश राज्य के रोजगार कार्यालय में जीवित पंजीयन).
  • Important: Candidates awaiting the final results of their prescribed educational qualification are NOT eligible to apply.

Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The selection process will involve a single-stage Computer Based Test (CBT), followed by Document Verification.

ModeOnline (CBT)
Exam StagesSingle Paper CBT, DV
Question TypeObjective (MCQ)
Negative MarkingNo (0 Marks)

CBT Exam Pattern

  • Duration: 3 Hours (180 Minutes)
  • Total Marks: 200
  • Part A (100 Marks): General Knowledge, General Hindi, General English, General Maths, General Reasoning, General Science, General Computer.
  • Part B (100 Marks): Subject-specific paper based on the post's required qualification.

Qualifying Marks

  • Unreserved (UR): 50%
  • SC / ST / OBC / EWS / PwD: 40% (10% relaxation)

How to Apply

  1. Candidates must apply online through the official MPESB website: www.esb.mp.gov.in.
  2. Aadhaar Registration is mandatory for all candidates.
  3. Candidates must have a valid and live registration with the MP Employment Office.
  4. Upload a recent color photograph (taken within 3 months, with name and date printed), signature, and a handwritten declaration as specified.
  5. Upload DOB proof (8th, 10th, or 12th marksheet) and category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwD) as applicable.
  6. Pay the application fee online and submit the form.
  7. Take a printout of the submitted application form for your records.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) for MPESB CEN 2025

1. What is the last date to apply for MPESB Group 2 Subgroup 3?

The last date to submit the online application is 12th November 2025.

2. Is there any negative marking in the exam?

No, as per the rulebook, there is no negative marking for wrong answers in this examination.

3. Is MP Employment Office (Rojgar Karyalaya) registration mandatory?

Yes. The rulebook clearly states that a live (valid) registration with the MP State Employment Office is mandatory for all applicants.

4. Can candidates from other states apply?

Yes, candidates from other states can apply, but they will be considered only under the Unreserved (UR) / Open category and will not be eligible for any reservation or age relaxation benefits.

5. What is the exam pattern?

It is a single 3-hour computer-based test (CBT) with 200 questions. 100 questions will be from general subjects (GK, Hindi, English, Math, etc.), and 100 questions will be from the technical/relevant subject for the post you applied for.

6. Is Aadhaar mandatory to apply?

Yes, the rulebook states that Aadhaar registration (आधार पंजीयन) is mandatory for all candidates.
