Bihar Technical Service Commission has released the advertisement for 493 Work Inspector (Mechanical) posts. Online Applications start from 12 December 2025.

Brief Description

Bihar Technical Service Commission (BTSC) invites online applications from eligible Indian citizens for the recruitment of Work Inspector (Mechanical) under the Public Health Engineering Department (PHED), Bihar. Candidates with Matriculation and relevant ITI Trade certificates can apply online.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

  • Matriculation (10th) Pass from a recognized Board.
  • AND
  • ITI Certificate in Machinist / Fitter / Wireman / Electrician Trade from a recognized Industrial Training Institute (NCVT/SCVT).
  • Note: Distance Education ITI certificates are NOT valid. Regular course required.

Exam Pattern & Syllabus (CBT)

The Computer Based Test will be conducted for 100 Marks (Time: 2 Hours). The paper will be bilingual (Hindi/English).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is the last date to apply for BTSC Work Inspector?

The last date for online application is 12 January 2026.

2. Which ITI trades are eligible for this post?

Candidates with ITI in Machinist, Fitter, Wireman, or Electrician trades are eligible.

3. Is there negative marking in the BTSC exam?

Yes, 0.25 marks will be deducted for each incorrect answer.

4. Can other state candidates apply?

Yes, candidates from other states can apply under the Unreserved (General) category.
