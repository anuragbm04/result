SSC Delhi Police Driver Admit Card 2025

Constable (Driver) Male in Delhi Police Examination, 2025

Last Updated: Loading...

✅ Sangam Result Update: Admit Card Released

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has released the Admit Card and Application Status for Delhi Police Driver Exam 2025. Candidates can download their Hall Ticket now.

Brief Description

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) is conducting the Computer Based Examination (CBE) for the recruitment of Constable (Driver) - Male in Delhi Police. Candidates who applied for this recruitment (737 Posts) can now check their Exam City and download their Admit Card for the exam scheduled on 16th & 17th December 2025.

Important Dates

Application Start24/09/2025
Last Date15/10/2025
Admit Card Out14/12/2025
Exam Date16-17 Dec 2025

Application Fee

Gen / OBC / EWS₹ 100/-
SC / ST / Ex-Servicemen₹ 0/-

Payment Mode: Online (BHIM UPI, Net Banking, Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, RuPay Credit or Debit cards).

Steps to Download Admit Card

  • Step 1: Click on the region-wise link provided below (e.g., NR, CR, MPR).
  • Step 2: Enter your Registration ID and Date of Birth.
  • Step 3: If you don't know your Registration ID, enter your Name, Father's Name, and DOB.
  • Step 4: Click on "Search Status" to view Exam City and Date.
  • Step 5: Download the Admit Card PDF (Available 4 days before exam).
