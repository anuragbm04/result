Brief Description- Bihar BSSC Inter Level Recruitment 2025

The Bihar Staff Selection Commission (BSSC) has released a massive recruitment notification for the 2nd Inter Level Combined Competitive Examination 2025. A total of 11,098 vacancies have been announced for various posts such as Lower Division Clerk (LDC), Revenue Staff (Rajaswa Karamchari), Panchayat Secretary, and others across multiple departments of the Bihar Government. This is a golden opportunity for candidates who have passed their 10+2 (Intermediate) examination. The selection process will consist of a Preliminary and a Main examination. Interested and eligible candidates are required to apply online through the official BSSC portal.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

  • Candidates must have passed the 10+2 (Intermediate) examination from a recognized board.

Technical Qualification

  • For posts like LDC, Typist, and Assistant Instructor, knowledge of computer operation and typing (Hindi/English) is required.

Exam Pattern & Selection Process

StagesPrelims & Mains

Duration2 Hours 15 Minutes
Negative MarkingYes, 1 Mark for each wrong answer

Preliminary Exam

  • This is a qualifying stage. Candidates five times the number of vacancies will be shortlisted for the Mains exam.
  • Syllabus:
    • General Studies: 50 Questions
    • General Science & Mathematics: 50 Questions
    • Mental Ability Test (Comprehension/Logic/Reasoning): 50 Questions

Main Exam

  • The Mains exam will consist of two papers: Paper-I (General Hindi) and Paper-II (General Knowledge).
  • Paper-I (Hindi) is qualifying in nature.
  • The final merit list will be prepared based on the marks obtained in Paper-II of the Main Examination.

How to Apply ( Bihar BSSC Inter Level Recruitment 2025)

  1. Candidates are required to apply online through the official BSSC website: https://bssc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Complete the online registration process with a valid mobile number and email ID.
  3. Log in and fill out the detailed application form with personal, educational, and other information.
  4. Upload a live photograph taken via webcam and scanned signatures in both Hindi and English.
  5. Pay the examination fee online.
  6. Review the application form carefully before final submission.
  7. Submit the final form before the deadline of 18/12/2025 and take a printout for future reference.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) for BSSC Inter Level 2025

1. What is the extended last date to apply for BSSC Inter Level Recruitment?

The last date for payment is extended to 15 Dec 2025, and the last date for final submission is 18 Dec 2025.

2. What is the educational qualification for BSSC Inter Level posts?

Candidates must have passed the 10+2 (Intermediate) examination or its equivalent from a recognized board. Some posts, like LDC and Panchayat Secretary, also require computer typing skills.

3. Is there a negative marking in the BSSC Inter Level Prelims exam?

Yes, there is a negative marking. For every incorrect answer, 1 mark will be deducted, while 4 marks are awarded for every correct answer.

4. Can candidates from states other than Bihar apply for this vacancy?

Yes, candidates from other states can apply for the Bihar SSC Vacancy. However, they will be considered under the Unreserved (General) category and will have to pay the application fee of ₹540/-.

5. What is the selection process for the BSSC 10+2 Level Recruitment?

The selection process consists of two stages: a Preliminary Written Exam and a Main Written Exam. The Prelims exam is qualifying, and the final merit list is prepared based on the marks obtained in the Mains exam.
