SSC GD Constable Notification 2025

Constable (GD) in CAPFs, SSF and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles Examination, 2026

Last Updated: Loading...

🔥 Sangam Result Update: SSC GD 2025 Notification Out

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has released the official notification for 25,487 Constable GD Posts. Applications are open from 01 December to 31 December 2025.

Brief Description

The Staff Selection Commission will conduct an open competitive examination for recruitment to the posts of Constable (General Duty) in BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, SSB, SSF and Rifleman (General Duty) in Assam Rifles. The recruitment process will consist of Computer Based Examination (CBE), Physical Standard Test (PST), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Medical Examination and Document Verification.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is the last date to apply for SSC GD Constable 2025?

The last date to submit the online application is 31st December 2025 (23:00 hours).

2. What is the exam date for SSC GD 2026?

The Computer Based Examination is tentatively scheduled for February - April 2026.

3. Is there negative marking in SSC GD Exam?

Yes, there is a negative marking of 0.25 marks for each wrong answer.

4. What is the age limit for SSC GD?

Candidates must be between 18 to 23 years as on 01-01-2026. Age relaxation applies for reserved categories.

5. Can 10th pass apply for SSC GD?

Yes, the minimum educational qualification is Matriculation (10th Class) pass from a recognized Board/University.
