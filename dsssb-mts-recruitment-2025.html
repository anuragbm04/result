Brief Description

Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) has released the Combined Examination, 2025 advertisement for the post of Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS). Recruitment will be held for various departments like Women & Child Development, Social Welfare, Training & Technical Education, etc., under the Government of NCT of Delhi.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is the start date for DSSSB MTS Online Form 2025?

The online application starts from 17th December 2025 (12:00 PM).

2. What is the age limit for DSSSB MTS?

The age limit is 18-27 years. Relaxations apply for reserved categories.

3. Is there negative marking in DSSSB MTS Exam?

Yes, 0.25 marks will be deducted for each wrong answer.

4. Can I apply for multiple departments?

A combined exam is held. You will submit preferences for departments at the time of e-dossier calling.
