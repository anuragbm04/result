Rajasthan RSSB Primary School Teacher (Level-1) Direct Recruitment 2025

RSSB REET Mains Primary Teacher Online Form 2025

Brief Description (RSSB REET Mains Primary Teacher Recruitment 2025)

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB), Jaipur, has released the detailed advertisement (Advt No. 08/2025) for the recruitment of **5636 Primary School Teachers (Level-1, Class 1 to 5)**. This massive recruitment drive covers posts in both the Sanskrit Education Department and the Elementary Education Department. Eligible candidates who have qualified REET Level-1 can apply online for these Rajasthan 3rd Grade Teacher posts. The online application process starts on 7th November 2025.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

  • Senior Secondary (12th Class): Passed with at least 50% marks.
  • Diploma (D.El.Ed/BTC): 2-Year Diploma in Elementary Education (by whatever name known).
  • REET Qualified: Must have qualified Rajasthan Eligibility Examination for Teacher (REET 2022 or REET 2025) for Level-1.
  • For Sanskrit Dept (Sanskrit Post): Varishtha Upadhyaya with 50% marks + D.El.Ed.
  • Note: B.Ed candidates are NOT eligible for Level-1 posts as per current rules.

Exam Pattern

  • Total Marks: 300
  • Total Questions: 150 (MCQs)
  • Duration: 2 Hours 30 Minutes
  • Negative Marking: 1/3rd mark deducted for every wrong answer.
  • Subjects: Geography, History, Culture of Rajasthan, General Knowledge, RTE Act, School Subjects, Educational Methodology, Psychology, IT.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) for RSSB Level-1 Teacher 2025

1. What is the last date to apply for RSSB REET Mains Level-1 2025?

The last date to submit the online application form is 06 December 2025.

2. When will the REET Mains 2025 exam be conducted?

The RSSB has scheduled the exam between 17 January 2026 and 21 January 2026.

3. How many total posts are there in this recruitment?

There are a total of 5636 vacancies for Level-1 Primary Teachers across Elementary and Sanskrit Education departments.

4. What is the age limit for this vacancy?

Candidates must be between 18 to 40 years of age as of 01/01/2026. Age relaxation applies for reserved categories.

5. Is B.Ed allowed for REET Level-1 (Primary Teacher)?

No. As per the notification, only candidates with a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed/BTC) are eligible for Level-1 posts.

6. Can candidates from other states apply?

Yes, candidates from other states can apply, but they will be considered under the General (Unreserved) category.

7. What is the application fee?

For General/OBC(Creamy Layer): ₹600/-. For OBC(NCL)/EWS/SC/ST/Divyang: ₹400/-. This is a one-time registration (OTR) fee.

8. Is there negative marking in the exam?

Yes, there is negative marking of 1/3rd mark for every incorrect answer.

9. What is the selection process?

The selection will be based on the marks obtained in the written competitive exam followed by Document Verification.

10. Do I need to be REET qualified to apply?

Yes, passing the Rajasthan Eligibility Examination for Teacher (REET) Level-1 is a mandatory eligibility requirement.
